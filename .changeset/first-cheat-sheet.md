Add a cheat sheet beside Blizzard's macro editor, with general macro reference and druid/healer examples. Highlight each example in blue with a + control to insert its exact text at the current macro cursor.

Insertion uses the native editor without forcing a save or executing a macro. It refuses unavailable/combat-restricted targets and insufficient space without truncating the draft; near-limit selections require shortening the draft first.

Keep the sheet clear of the icon picker when screen space permits.
